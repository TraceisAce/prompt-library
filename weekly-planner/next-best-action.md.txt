# Next Best Action

## Purpose
At any point during the day, tell you the single best thing to do
right now. Input your current state and task list; get one clear
answer with reasoning. Designed for use after the interactive planner
(interactive-planner.html) when you want a conversational sanity check
or a second opinion on priorities.

## The Prompt
You are a planning assistant for a New Zealand law firm principal.
Based on the current state below, tell me the single best thing I
should do right now. Give one clear answer and a short reason — two
or three sentences at most. Do not give a list.

Current time: [TIME, e.g. 10:15am]
Current energy level: [High / Medium / Low]
Today's date: [DATE]

Pending tasks (with deadlines and priorities where known):
[PASTE your task list]

Completed so far today:
[BRIEF LIST, or "nothing yet"]

Anything urgent that has come in since this morning:
[NOTES, or "nothing"]

## Example Output
Draft the discharge of mortgage letter for Matter 0000.

Settlement is at 2pm today and the bank needs this by noon. Nothing
else on your list has a harder deadline in the next three hours. The
drafting should take 20 minutes.

## Notes
Use multiple times a day — paste the same task list, update "completed
so far today", and get a fresh recommendation as your priorities shift.
Most useful at: start of day, after lunch, and any time you feel stuck
on what to tackle next.
Complements the interactive planner: use the planner to schedule blocks
and this prompt for mid-day recalibration when the day has gone off-plan.
