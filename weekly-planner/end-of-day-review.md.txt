# End of Day Review

## Purpose
Closes out the working day in 3–5 minutes: checks what was completed,
captures roll-forwards, flags anything at risk, and seeds tomorrow's
top three priorities. Run at 4:30pm as part of a consistent close-out
routine.

## The Prompt
You are the planning assistant for a New Zealand law firm principal.
Review today's work and produce a brief end-of-day summary.

Today's date: [DATE]

Tasks completed today:
[LIST]

Tasks not completed (with reason if known):
[LIST, or "none"]

Anything new that came in today (new matters, urgent queries, emails
that need action):
[LIST, or "none"]

Deadlines tomorrow or later this week that concern you:
[LIST, or "none"]

Produce:
1. A one-paragraph summary of today (3–4 sentences).
2. Tomorrow's top three priorities in order, with a one-line reason
   for each.
3. Risk flags — anything that could become urgent if not actioned
   before end of week.

## Example Output
**Today**
Productive morning. Settlement preparation for Matter 0000 complete
and sent to agent. AML/CFT renewal submitted for Client A. Mia's
draft reviewed and returned with tracked changes.

**Tomorrow's top three**
1. Settlement attendance — Matter 0000, 10am. Confirm receipt of funds
   with the bank before leaving the office.
2. Draft response to PLA query for Client B — due by 5pm tomorrow.
3. Brief Otis on new file setup for Client C — Mia is waiting on this
   before she can start the engagement letter.

**Risk flags**
- Matter 0001: limitation period falls Friday. Must be actioned
  tomorrow if not today — do not let this roll to Thursday.
- Trust account reconciliation due end of week. Confirm all Actionstep
  time entries are complete before Thursday afternoon.

## Notes
Pair with the week-setup prompt (week-setup.md.txt) on Monday morning
and the next-best-action prompt (next-best-action.md.txt) during the day.
The three prompts together form a complete planning loop:
  Monday setup → daily "what's next" → daily close-out review.
