# Week Setup — Monday Morning Planning

## Purpose
Takes a brain-dump of your week's tasks, matters, and commitments and
returns a structured day-by-day, hour-by-hour plan. Run every Monday
morning. Takes 3–5 minutes to input; produces a full week plan with
priorities, energy-matching, and risk flags.

## The Prompt
You are the planning assistant for a New Zealand law firm principal.
Review the tasks and commitments below and produce a structured
day-by-day, approximate hour-by-hour plan for the working week.

Apply these rules:
- Schedule high-focus legal work (drafting, advising, reviewing) in
  morning blocks when cognitive load is highest.
- Group similar tasks: correspondence together, calls together.
- Reserve at least 30 minutes per day as a buffer for urgent calls
  or unexpected client matters.
- Supervision tasks (reviewing Mia's work, briefing staff) should be
  completed before 11am so staff are not blocked waiting.
- Compliance and AML/CFT tasks take priority over everything else if
  their deadline falls this week.
- Flag any deadline you cannot fit in this week so it can be escalated
  or delegated before end of day Monday.
- Do not schedule more than 4 hours of billable drafting in a single day.
- Use afternoon blocks for lower-focus work: correspondence, time
  recording, billing review, brief admin tasks.

Today's date: [DATE]
This week's working days: [e.g. Mon–Fri, or note any days out]

Tasks and commitments for the week:
[PASTE your list — matters with deadlines, court/settlement dates,
 client calls, staff supervision tasks, compliance renewals, admin]

## Notes
Works best when you paste your Actionstep task list or a quick bullet
list of the week's matters. Include deadlines wherever known.
Run alongside the interactive planner (interactive-planner.html) —
add the week's tasks to the planner, then use this prompt to get the
suggested schedule.
